About

Researching economics
for a changing delta.

I am an economist and researcher from Bangladesh with an academic background in Economics from Khulna University, sitting at the edge of one of the world's most climate-exposed deltas.

My work lives at the intersection of environmental economics, development economics and public policy — with a particular focus on climate change, environmental vulnerability and rural livelihoods along the southwestern coast.

I'm interested in a specific question: how do environmental and economic shocks actually move through a household, and what would it take for policy to reach people before the shock does.
